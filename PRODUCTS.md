## 1. CCM Heavy Duty Mats

**High-Performance Contamination Control Flooring System**

Engineered to remove up to 99% of foot- and wheel-borne particulates within six steps, while helping reduce electrostatic discharge.

### 🌟 Key Benefits

- **Total Particulate Removal**: Proven to remove up to 99% of contaminants.
- **Electrostatic Proof**: Static dissipative barrier for sensitive electronics.
- **2-Year Guarantee**: Full replacement guarantee for performance reliability.

### 📏 Size Options

- **Widths**: 60cm, 90cm, 120cm, 150cm.
- **Lengths**: 90cm to 400cm standard, with custom shapes available.

## 2. CCM Classic Ergonomic Mats

**The Original Ergonomic Bubble Profile Mat**

Boasting the longest warranty in the industry, these mats have a track record of serving customers for over 20 years.

### 🌟 Key Benefits

- **Ergonomic Surface**: Stimulates blood circulation and reduces fatigue.
- **Beveled Edges**: Molded-in bevels that never curl or delaminate.
- **ISO Cleanroom Certified**: Rated for ISO Class 5 (Class 100).
- **8-Year Warranty**: Industry-leading protection.

### 📐 Versions

- **Classic**: 1.5cm (6") thickness.
- **Classic Deluxe**: 1.5cm (6") thickness.

## 3. CCM Infinity Ergonomic Mats

**Durable Polyurethane Ergonomic Mats**

Features a closed-cell surface that resists light moisture and most chemical exposures. Ideal for environments requiring ESD protection.

### 🌟 Key Benefits

- **ESD Option**: Electrical resistance < 10^9 Ohm.
- **Versatile Profiles**: Available in Deluxe, Smooth, and Bubble profiles.
- **Silicon Free**: Safe for sensitive manufacturing like paint booths.
- **6-Year Warranty**: Long-term reliability.

## 4. CCM Complete Ergonomic Mats

**Feature-Rich Industrial Mats**

Tolerates exposure to oils and liquids while providing enhanced anti-static protection and fire retardancy.

### 🌟 Key Benefits

- **Fire Retardant**: Qualified to EN 13501-1 standard (Self-extinguishing).
- **Chemical Resistance**: Handles mild exposure to oils and liquids.
- **Maximum Safety**: ESD versions available with precision resistance.
- **5-Year Warranty**: Robust protection for heavy-duty usage.
